The single word Grandma would text about AI is **Robutt** — her warm, slightly baffled attempt at typing "robot", misspelt exactly the way a grandmother thumbing out a message to her grandchild would misspell it, with the accidental "butt" landing the joke in one word and no caption needed. It reads unmistakably as a grandma text, it is funny on its own, it is tied to robots and AI rather than any subject at all, and it is a fresh pick rather than the obvious "robot" or "Alexa" — instantly readable, six letters, and exactly one word.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```

The program validates the word against the competition gate (UTF-8, 1–30 characters, not only white space, exactly one word with no white space inside) and writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.
