# Candidate words — One Word From Grandma

Judge brief (in priority order): (1) reads as the single word a grandma
would text about AI; (2) sounds like a real grandmother's texting voice,
warm and a little baffled; (3) funny in one word, joke lands with no
caption; (4) tied to AI / robots / machines, not any subject; (5) fresh
rather than obvious, still instantly readable.

## Candidates

1. **Robutt** — CHOSEN. Grandma's typo for "robot": the warm, baffled
   misspelling is exactly how she types, the accidental "butt" lands the
   joke instantly, it is unmistakably about a robot, and it is fresh
   (not the plain "robot"). Scores on all five criteria; also only
   6 bytes, which helps the size tie-break.
2. **Beepboop** — Strong runner-up. Grandma imitating robot noises is
   funny and clearly machine-tied, but it reads more like a sound effect
   she is quoting than a word she would actually text, so it loses to
   Robutt on criterion 2 (real texting voice).
3. **Witchcraft** — Grandma deciding AI must be witchcraft is warm,
   baffled and funny, but it is only loosely tied to AI (criterion 4):
   it would fit any mysterious new thing, not robots specifically.
4. **Terminater** — A grandma typo for "Terminator" is funny and
   robot-tied, but it leans on knowing the film, is colder and less
   warm than Robutt, and the joke needs the reference to land.
5. **Computerji** — Warm and specific (a grandma respectfully addressing
   the computer), clearly AI-tied, but culturally narrow: a judge
   unfamiliar with the "-ji" honorific may read it as a typo rather
   than a voice, so it loses on instant readability (criterion 5).
6. **Alexa** — Instantly grandma-like (she thinks AI *is* Alexa) and
   clearly AI-tied, but it is the single most obvious pick in the field
   (criterion 5 fails) and the joke is thin: it is a brand name, not
   a funny word.
7. **Halp** — The classic grandma typo for "help" is warm and baffled
   and funny, but it says nothing about AI at all (criterion 4 fails):
   it would fit a stuck jar lid just as well as a robot.

## Verdict

**Robutt** is the strongest: it is the only candidate that is simultaneously
a plausible grandma typo, funny without context, unambiguously about a
robot, fresh, and instantly readable — and the shortest of the strong
candidates.
